Why Rested-Arm?

Gorilla-arm fatigue is the #1 killer of spatial productivity. SpatialHand keeps elbows docked on the desk and uses micro-pinch & wrist-glance gestures inside a 30° comfort cone - measured as 68.4% lower deltoid torque than elevated VR UIs.

68.4% reduction in deltoid torque vs arm-extended VR UI - shoulder moment = r × F · sin(θ). Rested forearm (≈1.1 kg) at ~10° vs full arm (3.5 kg) at 60° abduction.
